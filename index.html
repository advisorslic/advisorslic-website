Career in LIC – A Trusted Path to Financial Freedom

A career in LIC (Life Insurance Corporation of India) offers one of the most stable, respected, and high-potential career opportunities in India. By becoming an LIC Agent or Bima Sakhi, you can build a long-term profession with unlimited income, lifetime renewal commission, flexible working hours, and strong growth prospects.

LIC is India’s most trusted insurance brand, providing agents with structured training, transparent commission systems, and continuous career advancement opportunities. Whether you are a student, working professional, homemaker, or someone looking for a reliable side income, an LIC agent career is suitable for both full-time and part-time work.

One of the biggest advantages of a career in LIC is financial independence. Agents earn through first-year commission, renewal income, bonuses, and performance-based incentives. Over time, this creates a stable monthly income and long-term financial security, especially in high-demand regions such as Delhi, Noida, and Ghaziabad.

By choosing a career in LIC, you not only secure your own future but also help families protect their financial well-being. This profession combines income, social respect, work-life balance, and personal growth, making LIC agency one of the best career options for long-term success.

Process to Become an LIC Agent LIC एजेंट बनने की प्रक्रिया

Step 1: Apply Online / संपर्क करें

Fill the Apply Now form or contact us on WhatsApp/Call. Guidance will be provided personally.

Step 2: Document Verification

  • Age proof (Aadhaar / PAN)
  • Education certificate (10th pass or above)
  • Address proof & photographs

Step 3: LIC Training

Mandatory training (online/offline) as per LIC process. Topics include insurance basics, ethics, and sales process.

Step 4: IRDAI Examination

Appear for the IRDAI exam. We provide preparation guidance and study material support.

Step 5: Appointment

After clearing the exam, you receive your agent code and can start working as per LIC guidelines.

Eligibility Criteria

LIC Agent

  • Minimum age: 18 years
  • Education: 10th pass (minimum)
  • Training & exam as per process

Bima Sakhi

  • Women candidates only
  • Age: 18 – 70 years
  • Minimum 10th pass
  • Monthly Stipend (as per program):
  • ₹7,000 per month – First Year
  • ₹6,000 per month – Second Year
  • ₹5,000 per month – Third Year

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले प्रश्न

Who can become an LIC Agent?

Any Indian citizen aged 18 years or above and minimum 10th pass can apply to become an LIC Agent.

LIC Agent बनने के लिए कितनी पढ़ाई जरूरी है?

LIC एजेंट बनने के लिए न्यूनतम 10वीं पास होना आवश्यक है।

How much income does an LIC Agent earn?

Income is primarily through commission and renewal income. Earnings depend on performance and consistency.

What is Bima Sakhi?

Bima Sakhi is a women-focused opportunity that can include stipend support (as per program) along with commission income.

Is LIC Agency part-time or full-time?

It can be part-time or full-time. You can work flexibly according to your convenience.

Is there any exam to become an LIC Agent?

Yes, after training you need to clear the IRDAI examination to proceed as per applicable guidelines.

How can I apply for LIC Agent or Bima Sakhi?

You can apply using the Apply Now form, WhatsApp, or by calling directly. Complete guidance is provided.
