## What is a City Career Agent (CCA) in LIC?

LIC City Career Agent (CCA) is a special opportunity offered by LIC for individuals who want to build a professional insurance career with additional support and benefits.

CCA agents work with focused business targets and growth opportunities in urban areas like Delhi, Ghaziabad, and Noida.

## Benefits of Becoming LIC CCA

### ✔ Monthly Allowance Support
Eligible CCA agents may receive performance-based allowance support as per LIC rules (Rs 7000/ month for First year).

### ✔ Unlimited Commission Income
Earn commission on policies sold along with renewal income.

### ✔ Professional Growth
Opportunity to build long-term career in LIC.

### ✔ Flexible Career
Work full-time or part-time according to your convenience.
